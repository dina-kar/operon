# Trademark policy

> **Status: the project's working policy, pending the owner's legal review.** It may change. Until reviewed, treat it as guidance, not a license grant.

"Loams", the Loams logo and related marks identify the Loams open-source project and the Loams Cloud service. The source code is licensed under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE), which does not grant trademark rights (section 6). This policy explains what you may do without asking.

## You may, without asking

- State the truth about your relationship to Loams: "built on Loams", "powered by Loams", "compatible with Loams", "runs on Loams".
- Use the name "Loams" in plain text to refer to the project, in docs, articles, talks and package descriptions.
- Distribute unmodified Loams releases under their own name.
- Name your own community packages descriptively, for example `loams-plugin-foo`, when it is clearly a plugin for Loams and not the project itself.
- Show the logo, unaltered, to link to the project or to say your product is built on it. Keep it no more prominent than your own brand.

## You may not, without written permission

- Name a fork, product, company, domain or service "Loams", or something confusingly similar ("Loams Pro", "Loamz"), or use the Loams logo as your own.
- Imply that the Loams project or Loams Cloud endorses, sponsors or is affiliated with your product, or announce a partnership we haven't announced.
- Distribute a **modified** build under the Loams name. Rename it, or ask us.

## Forks and products

A fork or commercial product needs its own name and logo. It can say "built on Loams" or "powered by Loams" next to that name. Example: "Acme Search, powered by Loams".
