# Deploy buttons

A "Deploy to <provider>" button runs Loams on a provider's infrastructure, BYOC, from a template in `deploy/<provider>/`. Templates are pinned, least-privilege, secret-free and tested.

Full convention and the button snippet: [docs/ecosystem/deploy-buttons.md](https://github.com/ostrium-labs/loams/blob/main/docs/ecosystem/deploy-buttons.md). Providers listed here once their templates merge: none yet.
